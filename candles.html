eighteen little wishes

18 CANDLES

Please click the candles to reveal the wishes :)

01

A place that feels like home

I hope you find a place where you don't feel the need to adjust yourself just to fit in. It could be a house, a city, a group of people, or even just a moment in your life. Wherever it is, I hope you recognize it when you find it.

Somewhere you don't have to adjust yourself to fit.

02

Unexpected adventures

I hope you do things that weren't part of the original plan. Some of the best memories probably won't come from the things you carefully prepared for, anyway.

For the plans that were never plans.

03

People who expand your world

I hope you meet people who introduce you to things you would've never found on your own. New places, new ideas, new ways of looking at things. It's always interesting how one person can make a world you thought you knew feel a little bigger just by being in it.

For worlds you haven't seen yet.

04

Beautiful coincidences

I hope you have more of those strange little moments where you think, "What are the chances?" I don't know if everything happens for a reason, but I do like the moments that make you wonder if maybe some things really are meant to cross our paths.

For the moments that make you wonder.

05

Slow days

I hope you have days where nothing is particularly urgent. Days where you can wake up slowly, take your time, stare at the ceiling for an unreasonable amount of time, or do absolutely nothing without feeling like you're wasting it.

For days that don't ask anything from you.

06

Wonder

I hope you continue to notice little things. Flowers, sunsets, a song you suddenly like, something funny someone said. Growing up tends to make people overlook things they once found interesting. I hope you don't let yourself become too used to the world.

For noticing what you could have missed.

07

Courage

I hope you have the courage to try things even when you aren't sure you'll be good at them. You don't have to know that you'll succeed before you decide something is worth trying.

For the things that frighten you before they become stories.

08

Discernment

I hope you also learn when to walk away. Not every opportunity is meant for you, and not everything you want is necessarily good for you. I hope you become comfortable enough with yourself to recognize the difference.

For knowing what to keep, and what to leave.

09

Laughter

I hope you never run out of things to laugh about. Especially the incredibly stupid things that probably shouldn't be as funny as they are.

For the incredibly stupid things.

10

Something to be proud of

I hope you accomplish something that makes you look back and realize just how far you've come. And when you do, I hope you actually stop and appreciate it instead of immediately looking for the next thing you have to accomplish.

For the moments you finally stop and look back.

11

Good surprises

I hope life surprises you sometimes. Not everything has to happen exactly the way you imagined for it to turn out well. Some things might even turn out better because they didn't.

Because not everything beautiful has to be planned.

12

Stories

I hope you collect stories. The ridiculous ones, the embarrassing ones, the ones you'll probably exaggerate when you tell them again years from now. I think a life is partly measured by the stories we eventually get to tell.

For the ridiculous ones you'll tell again.

13

Your sense of humor

I hope growing older doesn't make you take yourself too seriously. Keep the strange jokes and random observations. There's something nice about having things that make you laugh for no serious reason at all.

For the jokes that make no sense to anyone else.

14

Curiosity

I hope you remain curious. Ask questions just because you want to know. Learn things that have absolutely nothing to do with what you're supposed to be learning. I think there's something beautiful about wanting to understand the world simply because it's there.

For questions you don't need a reason to ask.

15

Little things

I hope you always have little things that make an ordinary day better. A favorite food, a song you keep replaying, a flower you happen to like, somewhere you want to go. They don't have to mean anything to anyone else. They're yours.

The things that seem insignificant, until somehow they aren't.

16

Excitement

I hope you never feel embarrassed about being excited over something. Even if other people don't understand why it matters to you. I think we're allowed to care about things simply because they make us happy.

You are allowed to care simply because it makes you happy.

17

A life that feels like yours

I hope you build a life that you actually want to live. Not one that simply looks good when other people look at it. At the end of the day, you're the one who has to wake up inside that life.

Not one that only looks good from the outside.

18

Room to become

I think this is probably the one I wish for you most.

I hope you give yourself room to become someone you haven't met yet.

You're eighteen. There's still so much you don't know, and I don't think that's something to be afraid of. You can change your mind. You can outgrow things. You can discover that something you once wanted isn't what you want anymore.

You don't have to have yourself figured out just because you've reached eighteen.

There's still so much life ahead of you, and I hope you let yourself grow into it at your own pace.

There are still versions of you you've never met.
